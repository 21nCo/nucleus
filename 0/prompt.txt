## Practical cut

If you are moving off `file:` to published versions, the high-confidence removals are:

1. The five `@superfunctions/oauth-*` packages on account
2. `@superfunctions/{auth,delivery,http-openapi}` on account
3. `@searchfn/{adapter-elasticsearch,adapter-contracts,core}` on account
4. `@datafn/core` on account, stores, products, and `client/data`
5. `@authfn/svelte` on components
6. `@secfn/core` on account
7. Root `@authfn/client` / `@superfunctions/{db,http}` once workspaces declare the published versions themselves

Those were local-link pins, not API usage.
